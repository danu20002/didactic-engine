# Chapter 01 Exercise

Write the answers in your own words. Simple English is welcome. Understanding is more important than perfect grammar.

## Part A — Explain

1. What is cybersecurity?
2. What is an asset?
3. What is the difference between a threat and a vulnerability?
4. What is the difference between authentication and authorization?
5. Explain confidentiality, integrity and availability with one example each.
6. Why is written authorization required before security testing?

## Part B — Classify

For each item, write whether it is an **asset**, **threat**, **vulnerability**, **exploit**, **control** or **impact**.

1. Customer database
2. Weak administrator password
3. Multi-factor authentication
4. Attacker tries leaked passwords
5. Loss of customer trust
6. Dishonest insider

## Part C — Apply to your work

Choose one application you built, such as a Spring Boot API, SAP BTP application or MotoBharat.

Identify:

1. Three assets
2. Three possible threats
3. Three vulnerabilities that developers should prevent
4. One preventive control
5. One detective control
6. One corrective control
7. One confidentiality requirement
8. One integrity requirement
9. One availability requirement

Do not include real passwords, company-confidential information or sensitive customer data.

## Part D — Risk scenarios

Complete this sentence three times:

> A ______ can exploit ______ and harm ______. We can reduce the risk by ______.

## Part E — Knowledge check

1. Is every vulnerability automatically an incident? Explain.
2. Can a backup improve confidentiality, integrity or availability? Explain the strongest connection.
3. A user logs in successfully but reads another user's record. Is this mainly an authentication or authorization problem?
4. Is a firewall always enough to protect an application? Why?
5. What should you do when testing may affect a system outside the approved scope?

Submit all five parts for review. Your technical understanding and English will be corrected before Chapter 02 is unlocked.
